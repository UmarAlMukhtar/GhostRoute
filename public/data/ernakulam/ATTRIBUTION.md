GhostRoute's MIT code license does not replace the data license. Follow ODbL attribution and applicable share-alike obligations when distributing or adapting the database.

The normalized source, acquisition URL, timestamps and hashes are bundled in `data/source/`. Runtime provenance and conversion exclusions are in `metadata.json`. Display “© OpenStreetMap contributors” with the copyright link in any map, including a tile-free local map. No map tiles or imagery are included.

Roads are real OSM geography, subject to incomplete mapping and assumed walking access. The fixture endpoints are demonstration locations, **not verified shelters**. Any later flood is simulated. Mapped routes are not verified safe or passable.
